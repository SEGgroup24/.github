Overall Look and Feel of UOboost
===============================
Minimalistic and understandably easy to operate for both new and experienced students/users.

Colours
=========
We decided to go with the uOttawa official colours (https://www.uottawa.ca/about-us/administration-services/brand):
Primary Garnet - HEX #8f001a
Charcoal Grey - HEX #2d2d2c
Polar Grey - HEX #f2f2f2
Primary Warm Grey - HEX #80746c
Primary Blue - HEX #636d77
(Optional if necessary): Dark Red - HEX #8B0000

Iconography
===========
- UOBoost name with an arrow going up, inspired by uOttawa but with our own style

Typography
==========
- Arial, sans-serif
- Titles: big and bold, garnet color
- Normal text: 16px

Buttons
=======
- Main button: garnet background, white text
- Second button: white background, garnet border
Overall: Flat design with simple calm colors.
