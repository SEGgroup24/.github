What uO Boost does

Product features

Four tools that help you find the right help and study with the right people.

Find a tutor

Search for tutors by course and get matched with the ones that fit your schedule, budget and learning style.

  • Filter by course code, like CSI 2110
  • See each tutor's grade in the course
  • Matched to your free hours

Rating and reviews

Every tutor has a star rating and reviews from students they've helped, so you know who's reliable.

  • 1 to 5 star ratings
  • Written reviews after each session
  • Top-rated tutors shown first

Study sessions

Book a session with a tutor or your group, pick a time everyone is free and reserve a room on campus.

  • Shared weekly availability
  • Rooms in Morisset, CRX, STEM and more
  • Reminders before each session

Study groups

Join a study group for your course or start your own, then invite classmates with matching schedules.

  • Groups for every course
  • Matched by courses and free time
  • Choose your group size
