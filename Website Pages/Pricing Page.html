One place to pay

No cash, no e-transfers to strangers. Everything goes through uO Boost.

1. Book

Pick a tutor and a time. You see the full price before you confirm.

2. Pay

Pay for every session in one place, and see all your payments in one list.

3. Rate

After your session, rate your tutor. The tutor is paid once the session is done.

Questions

Do I have to pay to use uO Boost?

No. Study groups, study sessions and matching are free. You only pay for tutoring.

Why do some tutors cost more?

A tutor's hourly rate goes up as their star rating goes up, so the best-reviewed tutors cost a bit more.

When does the tutor get paid?

You pay when you book, and the tutor receives the money after the session has happened.

Prices are in CAD and are examples for a student project. No real payments are taken.
